ArchX3D

二次元図面　→　三次元建築

A Python pipeline that turns 2D DXF floor plans into furnished, textured 3D buildings you can walk through in the browser. It drives headless Blender for extrusion and lighting, and places furniture with a candidate-scoring solver that ranks poses on wall affinity, door-swing clearance and walkable space.

Face Recognition Attendance

顔認証　出席記録

A desktop attendance system that identifies students from a live camera feed and logs each one exactly once per lecture hour — a repeat sighting inside the same hour is recognised and refused. Includes webcam enrolment for new students and writes a dated record out to CSV.

Fake News Detection System

多様入力　事実確認

A multimodal fact-checker that accepts an article as text, image or audio — pulling the words out with OCR or speech-to-text — then asks an LLM for a verdict. Certainty short-circuits; doubt escalates, fanning generated queries out to Wikipedia, news and web sources and appending what comes back.
